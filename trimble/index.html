Trimble R12i GNSS Receiver

Featuring Trimble ProPoint RTK engine and TIP inertial tilt compensation, allowing surveyors to capture accurate points without leveling the pole, even in challenging satellite conditions.

  • • Trimble TIP tilt compensation immune to magnetic interference
  • • Trimble ProPoint GNSS technology for 30% better performance
  • • Multi-constellation: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou & QZSS
  • • Rugged IP68 magnesium alloy construction

Trimble Robotic Total Stations

Trimble S-Series (S5, S7 & S9)

Robotic total stations engineered for speed, accuracy, and reliability. Equipped with MagDrive servo technology, SurePoint pointing correction, and Trimble VISION live video overlay.

  • • MagDrive electromagnetic drive for ultra-quiet, rapid pointing
  • • SurePoint automatically compensates for instrument settlement
  • • Trimble VISION video technology for remote measurement & documentation
  • • Seamless integration with Trimble Access field software

Self-Calibrating 3D Scanners

Trimble X7 & X9 Laser Scanners

Revolutionary 3D laser scanners with automatic survey-grade self-calibration, laser pointer georeferencing, and instant in-field scan registration in Trimble Perspective.

  • • Automatic calibration guarantees millimetric accuracy on every scan
  • • High-speed scanning: full HDR panorama and scan in under 2 minutes
  • • In-field automatic registration eliminates office post-processing delays
  • • Ideal for plant BIM, bridge inspection, and civil tunnels
