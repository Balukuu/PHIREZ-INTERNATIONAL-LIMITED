HOW WE WORK

Capability Spotlights

Illustrative examples of the type of work we deliver, built around real Phirez field photography — not named case studies with client-attributed results. Browse the full Field Gallery or get in touch to discuss your own project.

GEOSPATIAL & CADASTRE • Infrastructure Benchmark

National Geodetic Control & Infrastructure Cadastral Survey

The Challenge: Establishing reliable geodetic baselines across expansive, rugged terrain with intermittent cellular coverage — the kind of ground truth road construction and legal cadastre depend on.

The Solution: Multi-frequency Trimble R12i GNSS receivers running long-session dual-frequency static observations, tied to the International GNSS Service (IGS) reference frame and processed in Trimble Business Center (TBC) with least-squares network adjustments.

What This Delivers: High-accuracy control points suitable as the spatial foundation for engineering-grade infrastructure design and legal parcel cadastre.

Discuss Geodetic Solutions
MINING & AGGREGATES • Autonomous LiDAR Mapping

Aerial Drone Photogrammetry & Quarry Volumetric Auditing

The Challenge: Manual aggregate inventory reconciliation traditionally means surveyors climbing hazardous rock stockpiles with GNSS poles — slow, and a real safety risk.

The Solution: A DJI Matrice 350 RTK drone with a Zenmuse L2 LiDAR sensor flies automated terrain-following lines. Point clouds are classified in DJI Terra and cut/fill volumes calculated in Carlson Mining.

What This Delivers: Faster survey turnaround with no personnel exposed to unstable stockpile terrain, and volumetric outputs suitable for inventory reconciliation and audit reporting.

PRECISION AGRICULTURE • Multispectral NDVI Optimization

Multispectral Drone Imagery for Precision Crop Health Optimization

The Challenge: Commercial sugarcane plantations applying uniform blanket fertilizer treatment risk over-fertilizing fertile sectors while under-treating compromised blocks.

The Solution: High-resolution multispectral drone flights generate Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) analytics in ArcGIS Pro, producing prescription variable-rate application maps by block.

What This Delivers: Targeted, data-driven fertilizer application in place of blanket treatment — reducing input waste and supporting healthier, more consistent crop stands.

Discuss Precision Agri Solutions
CIVIL ENGINEERING • Corridor Stakeout & As-Built

Highway Corridor Stakeout & As-Built Verification

The Challenge: Contractors need to stake out centerline alignment points quickly while verifying earthwork layers against tight design tolerances to avoid costly rework.

The Solution: Trimble S-Series robotic total stations paired with Trimble TSC7 controllers running the Trimble Access Roading module, allowing one-person operation directly from CAD alignment files.

What This Delivers: Faster daily stakeout with one-person operation, and as-built verification data that supports compliance sign-off against design tolerances.

Discuss Highway Solutions
UTILITIES & GPR • Subsurface Asset Mapping

Subsurface Utility Line Tracing & Fiber Optic Strike Prevention

The Challenge: Expanding urban roads without reliable historical as-built drawings risks severing live power cables, pressurized water mains, and optical fiber lines during excavation.

The Solution: US Radar ground penetrating radar (GPR) coupled with Trimble Catalyst GNSS. Radargram cross-sections are converted into georeferenced 3D utility vectors.

What This Delivers: Georeferenced utility maps that reduce excavation risk and integrate directly into municipal spatial data infrastructure for future maintenance planning.
