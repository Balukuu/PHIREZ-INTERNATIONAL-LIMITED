GEOSPATIAL & CADASTRE • Infrastructure Benchmark

National Geodetic Control & Infrastructure Cadastral Survey

The Challenge: Establishing sub-centimeter geodetic baselines across expansive, rugged terrain with intermittent cellular coverage and severe atmospheric distortion.

The Solution: Phirez deployed multi-frequency Trimble R12i GNSS receivers executing long-session dual-frequency static observations, tied to the International GNSS Service (IGS) reference frame and processed in Trimble Business Center (TBC) with least-squares network adjustments.

The Result: Delivered millimetric coordinate repeatability across all benchmark control pillars, providing the authoritative spatial foundation for multi-million dollar road construction and legal parcel cadastre.

MINING & AGGREGATES • Autonomous LiDAR Mapping

Aerial Drone Photogrammetry & Quarry Volumetric Auditing

The Challenge: Monthly aggregate inventory reconciliation previously required surveyors to climb hazardous rock stockpiles with GNSS poles, risking personal safety and taking three full days per pit.

The Solution: Deployed a DJI Matrice 350 RTK drone equipped with a Zenmuse L2 LiDAR sensor and automated terrain-following flight lines. Point clouds were classified in DJI Terra and cut/fill volumes calculated in Carlson Mining.

The Result: Survey time was compressed from 3 days to 45 minutes of autonomous flight, with volumetric accuracy certified to within 1.5% and zero personnel exposed to steep quarry highwalls.

PRECISION AGRICULTURE • Multispectral NDVI Optimization

Multispectral Drone Imagery for Precision Crop Health Optimization

The Challenge: Commercial sugarcane plantations across Uganda were applying uniform chemical fertilizers, resulting in over-fertilization in fertile sectors and nutrient deficiencies in compromised blocks.

The Solution: Phirez executed high-resolution multispectral drone flights generating Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) analytics in ArcGIS Pro, creating custom prescription variable-rate application maps.

The Result: Reduced total chemical fertilizer consumption by 18%, targeted soil remediation zones with 95% precision, and delivered a measurable 12% improvement in crop yield at harvest.

CIVIL ENGINEERING • Corridor Stakeout & As-Built

Highway Corridor Stakeout & BIM As-Built Verification

The Challenge: Contractors needed to stake out hundreds of centerline alignment points daily while verifying earthwork layers strictly within ±10mm specifications to prevent costly road repaving penalties.

The Solution: Deployed Trimble S7 robotic total stations paired with Trimble TSC7 controllers running Trimble Access Roading module, allowing one-man operation directly from CAD alignment files.

The Result: Daily stakeout efficiency increased threefold. As-built verification point clouds confirmed compliance with UNRA design tolerances, eliminating contractor disputes and project delays.

UTILITIES & GPR • Subsurface Asset Mapping

Subsurface Utility Line Tracing & Fiber Optic Strike Prevention

The Challenge: Expanding major urban roads without reliable historical as-built drawings posed severe risks of severing live high-voltage power cables, pressurized water mains, and optical fiber lines.

The Solution: Mobilized US Radar Quantum Imager triple-frequency Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) coupled with Trimble Catalyst GNSS. Radargram cross-sections were converted into georeferenced 3D utility vectors.

The Result: Zero utility strikes during full excavation phases. Deliverables were integrated directly into the municipal spatial data infrastructure (SDI) for future municipal maintenance.
