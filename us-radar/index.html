Non-Destructive Subsurface Radar Imaging

From everyday utility locating to deep geotechnical investigation, US Radar systems detect what’s buried before you dig, drill, or design around it.

Subsurface Utility Engineering

US Radar Quantum Imager Triple-Frequency GPR

Ground penetrating radar capable of pulsing three frequencies simultaneously (250 MHz, 500 MHz, 1000 MHz) to detect metallic and non-metallic pipes, fiber optic cables, voids, and bedrock layers in a single pass.

  • • Stepped-frequency radar antenna technology for superior depth penetration
  • • Integrated GPS georeferencing exports directly to CAD and GIS layers
  • • Real-time 3D depth slice modeling eliminates excavation strike hazards
  • • Rugged all-terrain cart suited for African civil works sites
Inquire About Quantum Imager
All-In-One GPR/GNSS Mapping

US Radar GPRover

An all-in-one ground penetrating radar and GNSS mapping cart that fuses subsurface radar imaging with high-accuracy GPS positioning, so every detected utility or anomaly is captured with mapping-grade coordinates in a single pass.

  • • Integrated GNSS georeferencing on every radar trace
  • • Triple-bandwidth radar antenna for varied soil conditions
  • • Real-time on-cart data logging and depth-slice preview
  • • Direct export into CAD and GIS utility layers
Inquire About GPRover
Deep Geotechnical Imaging

US Radar 100 Series GPR

Modular, deep-penetration GPR systems built for geotechnical investigation, tunnel assessment, and bedrock or void mapping where standard utility-locating radar cannot reach.

  • • Penetration depth of up to approximately 30 metres, soil-dependent
  • • Modular antenna configurations for different depth/resolution needs
  • • Suited to tunnels, dams, and geological/geophysical surveys
  • • Pairs with Radar Studio post-processing software
