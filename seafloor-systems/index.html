Hydrographic Surveys & USVs

Seafloor Systems EchoBoat Autonomous Survey USV

Remotely operated and autonomous unmanned surface vessels (USVs) configured with singlebeam and multibeam sonar echosounders to map underwater topography, reservoir siltation, and river cross-sections.

  • • Autonomous waypoint navigation with collision avoidance
  • • Seamless integration with RTK GNSS for centimeter vertical water depth
  • • Siltation volume auditing for hydroelectric dams and water reservoirs
  • • Eliminates the safety hazard of putting manned survey boats in dangerous waters

Compact Portable USVs

Seafloor HyDrone & TriDrone

One-man-portable catamaran USVs that launch from a riverbank or small boat ramp in minutes, built for surveys where a full-size EchoBoat is impractical to transport or deploy.

  • • Hand-launchable and lightweight for remote sites
  • • Remote or autonomous waypoint operation
  • • Integrates with singlebeam echosounders and RTK GNSS
  • • Well suited to narrow rivers, canals, and small reservoirs
Inquire About Compact USVs
Portable Single-Beam Sounder

Seafloor HydroLite Echosounder

A lightweight single-frequency echosounder built from Seafloor Systems’ two decades of hydrographic sensor development, suited to depth profiling and siltation surveys on lakes, canals, and irrigation channels.

  • • Compact, low-power single-beam sensor
  • • Pairs with GNSS for georeferenced depth soundings
  • • Straightforward deployment from small boats or USVs
  • • Exports to standard hydrographic survey software
